=== 1. key expires on time ===
$ set k hello
expected: nil (from set)
got:      (nil) 

$ pexpire k 3000
expected: (integer) 1
got:      (integer) 1 

$ pttl k
expected: about 3000
got:      (integer) 2994 

--- sleep 4 ---

$ get k
expected: (nil)
got:      (nil) 

$ pttl k
expected: (integer) -2
got:      (integer) -2 

=== 2. extended TTL: stale entry skipped ===
$ set k hello
expected: nil (from set)
got:      (nil) 

$ pexpire k 1000
expected: (integer) 1
got:      (integer) 1 

$ pexpire k 60000
expected: (integer) 1
got:      (integer) 1 

--- sleep 2 ---

$ get k
expected: hello
got:      hello 

$ pttl k
expected: about 58000
got:      (integer) 57971 

=== 3. set clears the TTL ===
$ pexpire k 1000
expected: (integer) 1
got:      (integer) 1 

$ set k world
expected: nil (from set)
got:      (nil) 

--- sleep 2 ---

$ get k
expected: world
got:      world 

$ pttl k
expected: (integer) -1
got:      (integer) -1 

=== 4. whole sorted set expires ===
$ zadd board 1 a
expected: (integer) 1
got:      (integer) 1 

$ pexpire board 1000
expected: (integer) 1
got:      (integer) 1 

--- sleep 2 ---

$ zscore board a
expected: (nil)
got:      (nil) 

=== 5. pexpire on missing key ===
$ pexpire nosuchkey 1000
expected: (integer) 0
got:      (integer) 0 

$ pttl nosuchkey
expected: (integer) -2
got:      (integer) -2
